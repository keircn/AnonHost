FAQ

Q: Do I need an account?
A: No. Uploads are anonymous.

Q: How do I upload?
A: Use the form above or curl:
curl -F "file=@image.png" {{.BaseURL}}/api/upload

Q: How long are files kept?
A: Indefinitely, unless an expiration is set.

Q: Is there a file size limit?
A: Yes, {{.MaxFileSizeHuman}} per file.

Q: Can I delete a file?
A: Yes, using the deletion token from the upload response.
